# Sacred Logistics Tweaks (Forge 1.19.2)

**Sacred Logistics Tweaks** is a core gameplay extension and balancing mod specifically designed for the **Sacred Logistics** modpack. It introduces unique hardcore survival mechanics, custom world generation, and tight integration between popular exploration mods to deliver a highly immersive and challenging experience.

---

## Custom Mechanics & Challenges

### The Meteorite Hazard
* **Impending Doom:** Every 10 in-game days, a meteorite will target and crash down directly onto the player, leaving behind valuable custom ore.
* **Dynamic Scaling:** Survival gets slightly easier over time-the interval between meteorite strikes progressively increases according to a custom formula.
* **Protection:** Don't want to get blown up? Craft and carry a special **Meteorite Charm** to shield yourself and completely prevent crashes in your vicinity.

### Artifacts & Relics Integration
Survival now requires proper equipment. This mod adds strict penalties based on your gear:
* **No Flippers, No Swimming (*Artifacts*):** Trying to swim without **Flippers**? You have exactly 10 seconds before you start drowning. Keep your head above water or suit up!
* **Dimensional Lockdown (*Relics*):** Interdimensional travel is restricted. You cannot traverse between dimensions without possessing a **Spatial Sign**.
* **Social Etiquette (*Artifacts*):** Villagers are terrified of outsiders. Attempting to trade with them without wearing a **Straw Hat** will scare them off and cause the Iron Golems to instantly become hostile.
* And many other integrations with **tips** in the game!

---

## New Ores & World Generation

Explore new horizons and dimensions to find exclusive resources necessary for progression. All ores come with their respective raw versions and smelted ingots:

* **Chlorophyll Ore:** Generates naturally across the Overworld.
* **Demonite & Crimson Ore:** Hidden deep within the fiery depths of the Nether.
* **Cobalt Ore:** Found embedded in the floating islands of the End.
* **Luminite Ore:** An extremely rare resource generating exclusively on the icy surface of **Glacio**, the distant planet from the *Ad Astra* mod.

## FAQ

Q: Can I use this mod in my own modpack?

A: This mod was specifically tailored for **Sacred Logistics**. While you are free to include it in any modpack, please note that some mechanics heavily rely on the presence of *Artifacts*, *Relics*, and *Ad Astra* to function properly.
